JAYAM VIDHYA BHAVAN — RESPONSIVE SCHOOL WEBSITE
================================================

Included
- index.html: complete mobile-friendly single-page website
- assets/: optimized photographs, school gallery photos and supplied MP4 clip
- Responsive navigation, admissions enquiry form that opens WhatsApp, photo lightbox and video player

Preview
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Keep the assets folder beside index.html.
3. Open index.html in Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.

Publish to your domain
1. Open your hosting control panel / cPanel File Manager.
2. Open public_html (or the document root for your chosen domain).
3. Upload the contents of this ZIP, keeping the assets folder structure.
4. Visit your domain and test the menu, gallery, video and WhatsApp enquiry form on a phone.

Important checks before going live
- Confirm the school address, phone numbers, email addresses, subject groups, trust details and admission-year wording.
- The WhatsApp enquiry form currently opens +91 94422 20112.
- The website package is ready to upload, but it has not been published to your hosting account.
